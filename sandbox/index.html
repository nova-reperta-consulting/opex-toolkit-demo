What the scan found

Claims Handling measures a great deal and steers on almost none of it. The claims system refreshes overnight and nobody keeps a shadow spreadsheet to correct it, which is rare and worth protecting — but the numbers are read after the fact rather than used to decide the day.

The losses are not in the claim decisions. They are in moving information around them: the notification is typed twice because the contact-centre tool was never connected to the claims system, cover has to be read in a second system on every file, and customers phone to ask where their claim is because nothing tells them.

The team survey lands on the same place from the other side. Team spirit is at 96% favourable and workload at 32% — the one clearly negative statement. Neither of those is about the people.
